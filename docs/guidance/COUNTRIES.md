# Official guidance by country

Compiled 2026-11-08 from the official sources linked below. See [README.md](README.md) for how to read this and what it means for Vorrat; the same data is in [presets.json](presets.json).

Quotes are in the source's language. Quantities appear only where the authority gives a number. *Verified* means the numbers were read from the official source or an official copy of it; *partial* means only part of the list could be read; *unverified* means only the authority and link are known so far.

## Belgium <a id="be"></a>

| | |
|---|---|
| Authority | National Crisis Centre (Nationaal Crisiscentrum / Centre de crise national) |
| Source | <https://crisiscentrum.be/nl/wat-kan-jij-doen/maak-een-noodpakket> |
| Also read | <https://crisiscentrum.be/sites/default/files/documents/files/WEB-Brochure%20Samen%20voorbereid-NL.pdf> |
| Languages | nl, fr, de, en |
| Published | 2026-10-01 |
| Status | verified |
| Days | 3 days. 'minstens 72 uur zelfstandig'. |
| Water | 3 L per person per day. Between 3 and 10 L per person per day; 3 L covers only drinking and cooking. |

- **Water:** Water for drinking and cooking (3 L per person per day); Extra water for washing and flushing (3 to 10 L a day in total) (per person per day).
- **Grains and staples:** Crackers and energy bars.
- **Vegetables and pulses:** Canned vegetables and pulses.
- **Fruit, nuts, sweets:** Nuts and dried fruit.
- **Protein:** Canned meat or fish.
- **Medicine:** Personal medicines for at least three days; Iodine tablets, only if the authorities say so (free at the pharmacy).
- **First aid:** Small first-aid kit.
- **Hygiene:** Wet wipes, hand gel, toothbrush and toothpaste, toilet paper, sanitary pads.
- **Documents:** Copies of ID card, driving licence and insurance papers; emergency plan or phone list; pen and paper.
- **Cash:** Some cash.
- **Light:** Torch (wind-up preferred), candles and matches.
- **Power and batteries:** Spare batteries and power bank.
- **Radio:** Wind-up or battery radio (DAB+ and FM).
- **Cooking:** Can opener or pocket knife.
- **Warmth:** Warm clothes or blankets (per person).
- **Pets:** Cage or carrier, lead, food, water, pet passport, medication, waste bags (per pet, if: dog, cat, small_animal, bird).
- **Babies:** Baby food, nappies, toys (per baby, if: baby).
- **Grab bag:** Rucksack: water, biscuits, first aid, three days of medicines, whistle, power bank, document copies, cash (per person).
